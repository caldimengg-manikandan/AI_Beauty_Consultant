## 🏗️ How It Works (Technical Overview)

```
User uploads photo
       ↓
AI detects face (MediaPipe)
       ↓
Analyzes features (Custom algorithms)
  - Face shape (Oval, Round, Square, etc.)
  - Skin tone & undertone
  - Eye & hair color
  - Skin conditions
       ↓
Generates recommendations
  - Hairstyles matching face shape
  - Makeup colors matching skin tone
  - Nail designs & colors
       ↓
Displays results with visuals
```
